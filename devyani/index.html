Her Story

It started at a dining table.

No franchise playbook, no investors — just a teacher, four kids from the neighbourhood, and the stubborn belief that tuition could feel like family.

2018 — The dining table

Four students. One table. Zero shortcuts.

Devyani began teaching four neighbourhood students at her own dining table. The rule from day one: no rote formulas until the concept underneath actually made sense.

The years between — Word of mouth

Parents told parents.

No hoardings, no pamphlets. Batches filled because results came home in report cards and confidence came home in the kids. The dining table stopped being big enough.

Sector 19 — A real home

Tareshwar Tutorials gets its own address.

The centre moved to SCO 33, Main Market, Sector 19, Panchkula — proper classrooms, the same dining-table warmth. Batches stayed small on purpose, because that was never negotiable.

Today — Still in the classroom

Founder on paper. Teacher in practice.

Eight years on, Devyani still personally teaches every senior Biology class. In the 2025–26 CBSE boards, her students brought home scores like 92% — and she'll tell you the marks were never the point. They're the side effect.

“Concepts first. Marks follow.”
— Devyani Gautam
No. 01

Patient with slow questions

The question a student is scared to ask is usually the one holding everything else back. Devyani's classes make room for it — every time, without the eye-roll.

No. 02

Demanding on the basics

Warm doesn't mean soft. Fundamentals get drilled until they're reflexes, because everything in Class XII Biology stands on what should have been settled in Class IX.

No. 03

Small batches, on purpose

Growth was never the goal — knowing every student's weak chapter was. Batches stay small enough that nobody gets to hide in the back row.
